HIGHLIGHTS  (3-5 bullet points, each <= 85 characters)
==========================================================

We identify global-DoF contamination as a methodological pitfall in 2D-driven mesh benchmarks.

We propose PRP, a method-agnostic, pelvis-subtraction evaluation protocol that isolates pose recovery.

We release CPU-only SMPL-X fitters: a raw-pose variant and a SMPLify-X+VPoser variant under MIT licence.

On AGORA val the two fitters tie on 3D pose under PRP but VPoser yields visibly more natural bodies.

QED compresses reconstructed meshes by 10x for only 14.3 mm geometric error.
